CHÉRIE

Year 2026
Role Brand Design
Client Blender

CHÉRIE is a youth-focused hygiene brand built around softness, self-care, and everyday comfort. Rooted in the playful yet gentle essence of cherry, CHÉRIE transforms something as ordinary as soap into a moment of care, confidence, and self-expression.

Designed primarily for students and young adults, CHÉRIE speaks to people who want to feel clean, fresh, and confident—without harsh chemicals or intimidating “clinical” branding. It’s approachable, cute, and emotionally resonant, while still being trustworthy for daily hygiene.
