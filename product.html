CHÉRIE

Year 2026
Role Identity & Packaging
Client CHÉRIE

CHÉRIE is a lifestyle hygiene brand reimagined for the modern youth. Built on the pillars of softness, intentional self-care, and daily ritual, CHÉRIE transforms the mundane into a moment of sensory delight.

Replacing the "clinical" coldness of traditional hygiene with a vibrant, approachable aesthetic, the brand targets a demographic of Gen Z and young professionals who value products that are as shelf-worthy as they are effective.

Brand Overview

CHÉRIE is crafted around the core values of softness and everyday comfort. The visual identity evokes the gentle sweetness of cherries—a theme that underpins both the product aesthetics and brand personality. The concept is anchored in emotional resonance, appealing to audiences who seek personal care that feels like a treat.

The Objective

The mission was to disrupt a sterile, commodity-driven category by injecting personality and warmth. I aimed to build an authentic, engaging brand identity for CHÉRIE that stands out in a category often dominated by "clinical" cues, ensuring it felt relatably vibrant without sacrificing credibility.

Strategic Approach

Target Audience Alignment

Understanding that modern consumers value self-expression, the brand voice emphasizes joy and simplicity. We focused on the emotional connection younger audiences seek in lifestyle brands, positioning hygiene as an act of self-love.

Visual & Sensory Identity

A soft, warm palette of cherry reds and natural tones reinforces a sense of tenderness. Rounded shapes and minimalist illustrations convey approachability, while a modern layout ensures the brand feels premium enough for high-end retail environments.

Iconic Packaging

Every bottle and box is designed as a collectible piece. By prioritizing bold, fruit-forward graphics and tactile simplicity, the packaging is optimized for social-first platforms like Instagram and TikTok, turning every purchase into an opportunity for digital sharing.

Brand Positioning

CHÉRIE stands at the intersection of:

  • Reliably Playful: Bridging the gap between whimsical design and essential daily care.
  • Routine-Centric: Transforming "getting clean" into a sensory ritual.
  • Social-Ready: Aesthetics built for Gen Z discovery and organic brand advocacy.
