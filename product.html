RECUP

Year 2024
Role Brand Design
Client RECUP

RECUP is a sustainable cup rental system designed to reduce single-use cup waste in urban environments. The brand identity reflects the circular economy concept through thoughtful typography and a color palette inspired by nature.

The visual system includes a flexible grid, iconography representing the cup lifecycle, and warm, earthy tones that communicate sustainability while maintaining a modern, professional aesthetic.
