CHÉRIE

Year 2026
Role Brand Design
Client CHÉRIE

CHÉRIE is a youth-focused hygiene brand built around softness, self-care, and everyday comfort. Rooted in the playful yet gentle essence of cherry, CHÉRIE transforms something as ordinary as soap into a moment of care, confidence, and self-expression.

Designed primarily for students and young adults, CHÉRIE speaks to people who want to feel clean, fresh, and confident—without harsh chemicals or intimidating “clinical” branding. It’s approachable, cute, and emotionally resonant, while still being trustworthy for daily hygiene.

Brand Overview

CHÉRIE is a youth-focused hygiene brand crafted around the core values of softness, self-care, and everyday comfort. The name and visual identity evoke the playfulness and gentle sweetness of cherries — a theme that underpins both product aesthetics and brand personality. The concept is anchored in emotional resonance, appealing especially to students and young adults who value approachable, trustworthy, and aesthetically pleasing personal care products.

Project Goal

The primary objective of this project was to build an authentic, engaging brand identity for CHÉRIE that stands out in a category often dominated by "clinical" or traditionally sterile aesthetic cues. The design needed to feel friendly, vibrant, and relatable, without sacrificing credibility or product trustworthiness.

Design Strategy

Target Audience Alignment

CHÉRIE’s core demographic includes Gen Z and young Millennials looking for skincare products that feel personal and expressive — not just functional. The brand voice and visuals emphasize joy, simplicity, and everyday self-love, reflecting the emotional connection younger audiences seek in lifestyle brands.

Visual Identity

A soft, warm palette inspired by cherries and gentle natural tones reinforces a sense of comfort and tenderness. Rounded shapes and playful illustrations convey approachability while still maintaining a clean, modern look suitable for retail packaging.

Packaging Design

The product boxes and bottle visuals showcase fun, fruity graphics with a focus on simplicity and clarity. Packaging is designed to feel collectible — encouraging repeat purchases and social sharing, particularly on image-driven platforms like Instagram and TikTok.

Brand Positioning

CHÉRIE positions itself as:

  • Youthful yet reliable — bridging the gap between playful design and daily use products.
  • Emotionally resonant — focusing on self-care routines rather than just hygiene.
  • Social-ready — packaging and branding that perform well in digital spaces, important for Gen Z engagement.

This positioning supports brand loyalty, encourages shareability, and enhances both initial discovery and repeat purchase behavior.
